# Model family logo references

These marks identify the model publisher or family in `model-family-comparison.svg`.
They remain in their supplied brand colors.

- **Llama:** Meta infinity mark, checked against the
  [Meta Llama page](https://www.llama.com/) and the
  [Meta Llama Hugging Face organization](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama).
- **Mistral:** gradient pixel emblem, checked against
  [Mistral's official brand assets](https://mistral.ai/brand/).
- **Qwen:** Qwen hexagonal mark, checked against the
  [official Qwen organization](https://huggingface.co/Qwen).
- **DeepSeek:** whale mark, checked against the
  [official DeepSeek organization](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai).
- **Gemma:** gradient Gemma mark, checked against the
  [official Google DeepMind Gemma page](https://deepmind.google/models/gemma/).
- **Phi:** Microsoft four-square mark because Microsoft does not publish a
  separate Phi family symbol; checked against the
  [official Phi page](https://azure.microsoft.com/products/phi).

The compact SVG references were obtained from `@lobehub/icons-static-svg`
and retained locally so the generated comparison SVG can inline the exact
paths and remain self-contained. The PNG source captures are retained where
the official project organization exposed a distinct profile treatment.

All names and marks are trademarks of their respective owners and are used
only to identify the model families.
